# The job wait (main+0x6df19c), which the frame limiter (main+0x6aafd8, through main+0x6e0598) enters every frame, polls
# whether the jobs it waits for are done and sleeps 1 ms between polls in its mode 1 (main+0x6df47c: the sleep's length in
# w0, 1,000,000 ns). At 30 fps that is 3 % of a frame; at 100 fps and above it is a tenth of one on the main thread, spent
# after the jobs have mostly finished. 200 us (0x30d40) polls more often; the wait stays a poll, so nothing else changes.

# version of this site replaced only the movz, and the movk still wrote 0x4240, so it slept 213,568 ns.)
